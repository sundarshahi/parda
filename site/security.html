Security

What parda protects against, what it doesn't, and what it can touch on your machine.

What it protects against

  • Secrets and personal data in your prompts, files, logs and tool output reaching an AI company or a proxy in between.
  • Those values being stored, logged, used for training or exposed in a breach on the AI company's side — they never had them.

What it doesn't protect against

  • Data parda's rules don't recognise. See known limits.
  • Images, PDFs and audio sent inside requests.
  • Tools that don't route through parda (see Supported tools), or a tool you point elsewhere yourself.
  • Someone who can already run programs as you on your machine. They could read your files directly.
  • Telemetry a tool sends to its own maker, separately from its AI requests.
  • Request headers. They are forwarded as they are, because they carry your API key to the provider.

What parda can do on your machine

  • Runs as your user. Never asks for root.
  • Listens on 127.0.0.1 only — not reachable from other machines.
  • Makes no network connections of its own: it only forwards the requests your tools make.
  • Writes the files listed on the install page, each marked so uninstall can remove it.
  • install.sh downloads a release from GitHub and checks its SHA-256 checksum before running it.

Report a vulnerability

Please report privately: on GitHub, open the repository's Security tab → Report a vulnerability. Don't open a public issue. You'll get a reply within a week, and a fix or a plan within 30 days.
