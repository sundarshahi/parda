For compliance teams

parda is one technical control for AI use: it keeps sensitive data from leaving staff machines in AI requests, and records that it did.

parda doesn't make you compliant. No tool does. It gives you a control to describe in your policies and evidence to show an auditor. Whether it is enough for a given control is your auditor's call.

The evidence log

Every request through parda adds one JSON line to ~/.parda/log.jsonl. It records what kinds of data were masked and how often — never the values.

{"time":"2026-11-21T16:50:58Z","api":"anthropic","method":"POST","path":"/v1/messages","status":200,"masked":{"EMAIL":3,"PERSON":1,"SECRET":1},"restored":3,"mask_us":7938}
FieldMeaning
timeUTC, ISO 8601
api, method, pathWhich AI service and endpoint the request went to
statusThe answer's HTTP status (parda's own refusals are 4xx/5xx with a parda error)
maskedCount per kind: SECRET, PERSON, EMAIL, PHONE, CARD, IBAN, SSN, IP, ID
restoredHow many stand-ins were put back in the answer
mask_usTime spent masking, in microseconds

The file is rotated at 10 MB, keeping one old file. For longer retention, ship it to your log system — each line is standalone JSON.

How it maps to common frameworks

FrameworkControlWhat parda contributes
ISO/IEC 27001:2022A.8.11 Data maskingPseudonymization and tokenization of personal data and secrets before transfer to AI services; masked samples via parda mask.
A.8.12 Data leakage preventionA control on one leakage channel (AI requests), with rules versioned in source control and a log of what was caught.
A.5.14 Information transferSensitive values are not transferred to the AI provider at all.
SOC 2 (Trust Services Criteria)CC6.7 Restricts transmission of informationRestricts what leaves in AI requests; fails closed when it can't check.
CC7.2 MonitoringPer-request records of detections, for sampling across the period.
GDPRArt. 25 data protection by design; Art. 32 security (pseudonymisation)Pseudonymisation before transfer, with the re-identification table kept on the exporter's machine, separate from the processor — the separation the EDPB's draft Guidelines 01/2025 on pseudonymisation describe.
Nepal Individual Privacy Act 2075Purpose limitationPersonal data collected for HR or customer service isn't handed to an AI provider in identifiable form.

Two things to keep in mind:

  • Pseudonymised data is still personal data under GDPR. parda reduces what you transfer; it doesn't take the transfer out of scope.
  • It isn't HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification. parda doesn't detect all 18 Safe Harbor identifiers (for example, addresses and dates).

Rolling it out

  1. Install parda on each machine that uses AI coding tools (one command, no settings).
  2. For Claude Code, use managed settings to fix the base URL so it can't be bypassed (Anthropic's gateway docs).
  3. Collect ~/.parda/log.jsonl into your log system.
  4. Record parda in your Statement of Applicability under A.8.11/A.8.12, and in your AI-use policy.
  5. Track false finds and misses as you would DLP incidents. Report rule problems with made-up examples on GitHub.

What's not there yet

Central policy and a tamper-evident (hash-chained) log are not in this release. The rules are versioned in the open source repository, so every change has history and review.
